Before you set off

A few useful details for planning your rental.

Where do I pick up my ride?

Pickup is in Siliguri. Confirm your pickup time and location with our team on WhatsApp before you travel. Find ABHI Bike Rental on Google Maps.

How do I confirm a booking?

Use our booking page to choose a ride and enter your trip details. Send the enquiry on WhatsApp, where our team confirms availability, pricing and pickup arrangements.

Can you help me choose a ride?

Yes. Tell us where you're heading, your rental dates and your riding experience. We'll help you check the available options for your plans.

What should I check before pickup?

Confirm the required documents, rental terms, deposit, fuel policy and helmet arrangements with our team before collecting your ride.
